# Stranger Things

## Why It Is My Favorite Series

*Stranger Things* is one of my favorite series because it is very exciting and emotional at the same time. The story is not only about monsters and supernatural events, but also about friendship, family, and teamwork. Every episode makes me curious about what will happen next.

## Things I Like About the Series

- The 80s theme and music
- The friendship of the characters
- The suspense and plot twists
- The emotional scenes
- The mix of horror, comedy, and action

## Favorite Season

### Season 4

I think Season 4 is the best season because the story became darker and more intense. The villain **Vecna** was very scary and the action scenes were amazing. The acting of the characters also improved a lot.
